Yearly Leave Compensation Policy

Unused leave days (Vacation, Sick, Emergency, Bereavement) are compensated at year-end. Maternity and Paternity leaves are non-convertible.

Formula: Unused Days × Employee's Daily Rate = Yearly Compensation Amount

Example: 5 unused vacation days × ₱1,000/day = ₱5,000 yearly compensation
